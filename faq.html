FORGEBOX FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ForgeBox custom ammo boxes, from materials and customization to Stack Codes, production, shipping, and wholesale.

Getting Started

What is a ForgeBox?

A ForgeBox is a premium, made-to-order ammo storage box designed around specific calibers, capacities, colors, and optional custom text.

Are ForgeBox ammo boxes 3D printed?

Yes. ForgeBox ammo boxes are 3D printed and built to order with caliber-specific fit, custom color options, and precision-printed detail.

What materials do you use?

ForgeBox products are made from premium PLA, a strong, durable plastic ideal for custom ammo storage. Every ForgeBox is built using production settings refined through dozens of iterations to maximize print quality. Delivering crisp details, a premium finish, and exceptional dimensional accuracy. It's lightweight, won't rust or corrode like metal, and is built to keep your ammunition organized at home, on the bench, or at the range.

Are ForgeBox products made in the USA?

Yes. ForgeBox products are designed, printed, assembled, inspected, and shipped from New Hampshire.

Customization

What calibers are available?

ForgeBox offers a growing lineup of pistol, rifle, and shotgun calibers. Available options can be selected directly in the ForgeBox builder.

Can I customize the colors?

Yes. You can choose custom color combinations for your ForgeBox using our builder. This includes the box/lid and text.

Can I add custom text?

Yes. Many ForgeBox configurations include optional custom text for a name, label, load note, or other short personalization.

What if I don't see my caliber?

Contact us if you don't see the caliber you're looking for. New calibers are added over time, and we may be able to help with a future option or custom request.

Quality & Production

How durable are ForgeBox ammo boxes?

ForgeBox products are designed for organized ammo storage and normal handling. They are sturdy and practical, but they are not intended to be waterproof, crush-proof, or used as hard protective cases.

How long does production take?

ForgeBox products are made to order and typically ship in 3–5 business days.

Are these mass produced?

No. ForgeBox products are built to order instead of pulled from a warehouse shelf. Each order is printed, assembled, and inspected before shipping.

Ordering & Shipping

How does shipping work?

Shipping is calculated at checkout. Orders over $70 qualify for free shipping.

What is your return policy?

Because every ForgeBox is custom made to your specifications, we generally cannot accept returns for products that were ordered correctly. If your order arrives damaged, contains a manufacturing defect, or we made a mistake, we'll make it right. If you have an issue with your order, simply contact us and we'll work with you to find the best solution.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Because every ForgeBox is built to order, changes or cancellations can usually be made before production begins. Once printing has started, we may no longer be able to modify the order. If you need to make a change, contact us as soon as possible.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes. ForgeBox offers wholesale opportunities for retailers, ranges, shops, and other businesses interested in carrying custom ammo storage products.

Stack Codes

What do the Stack Codes mean?

Stack Codes help identify which ForgeBox sizes are designed to stack together, making it easy to build a clean, organized storage system as your collection grows.

Want to learn more? Visit our Stack Codes Guide for examples and a complete explanation of how the system works.

Are rifle and pistol boxes stackable?

Some boxes are stackable with others depending on their size and Stack Code. Check the Stack Code shown with each capacity to see which boxes are designed to work together.
