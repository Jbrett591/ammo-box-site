ABOUT FORGEBOX

Premium ammo storage for people who appreciate quality firearms and accessories.

ForgeBox was started with a simple idea: ammo storage should reflect the same attention to detail as the firearms and gear it sits beside.

Why ForgeBox Exists

I'm a firearms enthusiast who has always appreciated the details. My safe used to be a collection of mismatched factory ammo boxes, generic plastic containers, and ammo cans. They worked, but they never felt organized.

When I discovered the idea of customizable ammo storage, I saw an opportunity to build a business around a product I genuinely appreciated. ForgeBox was created to make those products available to other firearms enthusiasts who value organization, personalization, and attention to detail.

Today, every ForgeBox is printed to order, assembled, inspected, and shipped from New Hampshire. Each order is built around the caliber, capacity, colors, and personalization selected by the customer.

Our Approach

ForgeBox isn't a large manufacturing company, and we don't want to be.

We're a small American business that believes doing a few things exceptionally well is better than trying to do everything.

That means focusing on consistent print quality, careful assembly, thorough inspection, and treating every order like it's being built for our own workbench.

Whether you're organizing a safe, setting up a reloading bench, preparing for a trip to the range, or simply looking for a better way to store your ammunition, our goal is the same: build something you'll enjoy using every time you reach for it.

Built to Order

Every ForgeBox begins production after your order is placed.

We don't keep shelves full of finished inventory. Each order is printed, assembled, inspected, and packaged specifically for the person who ordered it.

That also means you can choose the caliber, capacity, colors, and optional custom text that fit your needs.

No two firearm collections are exactly alike, and we don't believe your ammo storage has to be either.

Our Commitment

There are plenty of ways to store ammunition.

Our goal isn't simply to provide another box.

It's to provide a product, and a customer experience, that reflects the same pride many people take in their firearms, accessories, and equipment.

From your first visit to ForgeBox to the day your order arrives, we're committed to clear communication, attention to detail, and building products we're proud to put our name on.
