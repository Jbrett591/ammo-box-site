ForgeBox Web Standards v1.0
Purpose

Every page added to ForgeBox should follow these standards to ensure:

Maximum search engine visibility
Consistent branding
Professional social sharing
Future scalability
Easy maintenance

These standards apply to every page unless otherwise noted.

1. Technical Standards

Every page must include:

✅ Google Analytics (GA4)

gtag.js

✅ Title

Unique.

Primary keyword first.

Brand last.

Examples

Custom Ammo Boxes | 3D-Printed Ammo Storage | ForgeBox Co.

Custom 12 Gauge Ammo Box | Gray & Black | ForgeBox

✅ Meta Description

140–160 characters

Natural language

Contains primary keyword

Contains ForgeBox

✅ Canonical

Every page.

https://www.forgeboxco.com/page.html

11. Lessons Learned

Every time we discover something that works exceptionally well—or something that doesn't—we add it here.

For example:

June 2026: Google indexed individual build pages and images much faster than expected. Continue creating standalone build pages instead of one large gallery.
June 2026: Canonical tags added to every page after initial launch to reinforce preferred URLs.
June 2026: Real customer photography consistently outperformed generic product shots in search appearance and social engagement.

This turns the document into more than a checklist. It becomes the history of how ForgeBox's online presence evolved and captures the reasoning behind decisions. Six months from now, we won't have to wonder why we chose a particular approach—we'll have it documented.
